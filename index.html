About Us

Growing Quality, Delivering Freshness

A tomato-focused farming business with a vision for modern, sustainable agriculture.

I.E. Tomagrove

Based in Kigali, Gasabo, Rutunga, Rukerereza, I.E. Tomagrove is a growing tomato farming business specializing exclusively in tomato production and fresh tomato supply. Led by CEO IGIRIMBABAZI ERIC, we are committed to quality, reliability, and building lasting relationships with our customers.

Our Mission

To grow and supply quality tomatoes while building a reliable, responsible, and sustainable tomato farming business.

Our Vision

To become a trusted tomato producer and supplier in Rwanda by improving farming practices, product quality, and customer relationships.
